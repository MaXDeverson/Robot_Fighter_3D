Privacy Policy

Street Fighter Robot Boxing · Neonwhitepink · Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Street Fighter Robot Boxing (the "Game") and the third-party services built into it collect, why, and what choices you have. The Game is developed and published by Neonwhitepink ("we", "us").

  • The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location permission.
  • Your game progress, coins, unlocked robots and levels, and sound settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob and measures how it is played through GameAnalytics. These services collect device identifiers and usage data.
  • Optional in-app purchases are paid through Google Play. We never see your card or billing details.
  • Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states) the Game asks for your choice first, and you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy options.

1. Information stored on your device

Your level progress, in-game coins, unlocked robots and levels, the items you bought (such as ad removal) and your sound and music volume are saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

3. How we use information

We use the information above only to show ads that pay for the free Game, to deliver in-app purchases, to improve levels and difficulty, and to find and fix errors. We see analytics only as statistics in the GameAnalytics dashboard. We do not try to identify you personally, and we do not combine this data with other sources.

4. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. On first launch, before any ads or analytics start, the Game shows a consent form from Google. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes and partners. If you refuse, the Game still works fully: ads are limited and non-personalized, and analytics are not collected.
  • US states with privacy laws. You can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising through the same form.
  • Change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options in the Game. The button is shown where the law of your region gives you this choice.
  • Android settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).
  • In-app purchases are optional. The Game can be played in full without buying anything.

This policy is always available in the Game under Settings → Privacy policy.

5. Sharing of information

Information is shared only with the services listed in section 2, for the purposes described there. We do not sell your personal information for money. Under some US state laws, using advertising identifiers for personalized ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising"; you can opt out as described in section 4.

6. Data retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Google keeps a record of your purchases in your Google account. Google, GameAnalytics and Unity keep the data they collect for as long as their own policies state.

7. Security

The services above send data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we use providers that apply industry-standard protections.

8. International transfers

Google, GameAnalytics and Unity may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their privacy policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, the quickest way to stop collection is Settings → Privacy options or the Android settings described in section 4. For any other request, write to us at the address below and we will help as far as we and our providers can identify your data.

10. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game and data was collected, contact us and we will work with our providers to delete it.

11. Links to other services

The Game has buttons that open Google Play pages: the Game's own page (to rate it), our developer page, and our other games shown in the menu. Google Play has its own privacy policy; this policy does not cover it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or the services it uses change. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
